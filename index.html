A little bit about me

Hi, I'm Talia a creative with a background in music, photography, and visual storytelling.

I'm a professionally trained violinist with a 9-year diploma and have been freelancing since I was 16. Over the years, I've worked across creative fields, including appearances on TV, music videos and founder of a wedding studio based in Australia. I'm a fast learner, adaptable, and comfortable jumping into new environments and projects.

As a traveller at heart, I packed my bags and moved to Australia for a few years to explore, grow, and chase new experiences. Now based in Vietnam, I carry that same sense of adventure into everything I create.
